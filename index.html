How to play

  
Move with the arrow keys or WASD (swipe or use the pad on a phone).
     Every level is a build spec: fetch exactly the components it lists, walk through the
     assembly socket — it only opens once the spec is complete — and reach the
     power button before the clock runs out. Parts marked with a red cross belong to a
     different machine: picking one up costs you ten seconds.

  
Malware kills on contact — unless you are carrying a quarantine kit, which shuts
     one down and is used up doing it. There are never enough kits for every monster, so pick your fights.
     Practice in the top bar stops the clock while you learn a level; nothing is recorded
     from a practice run.

  
Made a mess of a crate push? Z rewinds one move, even the one that killed you
     — the clock keeps running, and a clean run with no rewinds is worth the third star.
     R restarts, P pauses. Every level has a par move count from the
     solver; beat 125% of it with no rewinds and no wrong parts for three stars.
     You can also build your own levels and share them as a link.
